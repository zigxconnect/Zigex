## 🎯 How To Actually Land These Internships

Okay, now you know WHERE to apply. But how do you actually get IN? Here's your game plan:

### Step 1: Build Something (Anything)
Before you apply anywhere, have at least ONE project you can show. It doesn't have to be perfect. It just has to exist.

### Step 2: Get On Zigex
Create your profile on [**Zigex**](https://zigexconnect.com). This is where many of these companies post opportunities. Having a complete profile makes you discoverable.

### Step 3: Attend Events
SEED, BitsValley, and others regularly host workshops and meetups. **Show up.** Be seen. Ask questions.

### Step 4: Apply Directly
Don't wait for postings. Visit their websites. Send thoughtful applications. Follow up (politely).

### Step 5: Be Patient But Persistent
Not everyone gets accepted first try. Keep learning, keep building, and keep applying.
