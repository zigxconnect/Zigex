# Zigex community guidelines — DRAFT for review

> Status: draft written for the Zigex team to edit and approve. Not published
> anywhere yet. Once approved, it can be shown on the Communities page (with a
> "Report a problem" link) and pinned in the Discord #information channel.
>
> Things the team must decide before publishing are marked **[DECIDE]**.

## Why these exist

The Zigex community is where students in Bamenda and across Cameroon ask
questions, share what they build and study together. Many members are young
and new to tech. These guidelines keep it a place where anyone can ask a
"basic" question without fear.

## Be kind and respectful

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their school, level, language,
  gender, religion, ethnicity or region.
- No insults, mockery of beginners, harassment or threats.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.

## Keep it safe

- Never share someone else's phone number, address, photos or personal
  details without their permission.
- Don't share your own passwords, Mobile Money PINs or verification codes,
  even with someone claiming to be from Zigex. Zigex staff will never ask
  for them.
- No sexual content, violence or content that is illegal in Cameroon.
- Be careful with "job offers" that ask you to pay first. Report them.

## Keep it useful

- Post in the right channel: questions in #homework-help, planning in
  #session-planning, everything else in #off-topic.
- No spam, chain messages, or repeated self-promotion. Sharing your own
  project once in the right channel is welcome.
- When you ask for help, say what you tried and paste the error message.
- Don't share pirated software, leaked exams or other people's work as
  your own.

## Programs and internships

- Program and internship group chats are for that cohort. Keep company
  information shared there private unless the company says otherwise.
- Follow the instructions of program organisers and supervisors in their
  groups.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks these guidelines or makes you feel unsafe:

1. Don't reply to them.
2. Take a screenshot if you can.
3. Report it: **[DECIDE: one place — e.g. a #report channel visible only to
   moderators, a moderator's Discord name, or an email such as
   zigexconnect.com@gmail.com]**.

Reports are read by the Zigex team **[DECIDE: e.g. within 48 hours]** and
kept confidential.

## What happens when guidelines are broken

**[DECIDE the steps; a common approach:]**

1. A private warning from a moderator.
2. A temporary mute or removal from the channel.
3. Removal from the community and, for serious cases, from Zigex.

Threats, sharing someone's private information, or scams lead straight to
removal.

## Recommended Discord / WidgetBot settings (for the server admin)

Not part of the published text — settings that make the rules enforceable:

- **Turn off WidgetBot guest posting**, so people must sign in with Discord
  to post (today messages show as "GUEST", which is anonymous).
- Slow mode on the busiest channels (e.g. 10–30 seconds).
- Discord AutoMod: block invite links, mass mentions and common spam terms.
- At least two moderators, including one available on weekends.
- Pin these guidelines in #information.
