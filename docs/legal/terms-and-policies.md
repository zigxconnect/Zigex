# Terms of Use

**Effective 8 October 2026**

These are the rules for using Zigex, for students, companies and visitors. They're written to be read; the short version comes first.

## The short version

- Zigex is for people aged 18 and over. Keep your profile and applications truthful.
- Companies decide who they accept; Zigex doesn't guarantee a place or a job.
- Fees, when an opportunity has one, are shown up front and paid to the company. Zigex never asks you to pay.
- Be respectful, don't post fake opportunities or spam, and don't copy other people's data.
- You own what you post and can delete your account at any time.

## 1. About these terms

Zigex is run by **SEED Inc**, Mawa, Mile 6 Nkwen, Bamenda, Cameroon ("SEED", "we", "us"). These terms apply when you use zigexconnect.com or the Zigex app, as a student, a company or a visitor.

By creating an account or using Zigex, you agree to these terms and to our [Privacy Policy](#privacy-policy). If you don't agree, please don't use Zigex.

## 2. Who can use Zigex

- You must be at least 18 years old.
- Give accurate information about yourself, and keep it up to date.
- One person, one student account. Company accounts must be opened by someone allowed to act for that company.

## 3. Your account

- Keep your password private, and use one you don't use elsewhere. You're responsible for what happens in your account.
- Tell us straight away at zigexconnect.com@gmail.com if you think someone else is using it.
- You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

## 4. Applying to opportunities

- Your applications and profile must be truthful: don't claim skills, qualifications or experience you don't have, or submit someone else's work as yours.
- When you apply, the company receives your profile, answers, documents and phone number (see [Who can see your information](#4-who-can-see-your-information) in the Privacy Policy).
- Companies decide who they accept. Zigex doesn't guarantee an interview, a place or a job.
- If you're accepted, follow the company's rules for the internship, program or event, including attendance and reporting.

### Programs and events with a fee

Some programs and events charge a fee, shown before you register. You pay the company that runs it, following its instructions; Zigex doesn't take payments. Refunds, if any, follow that company's policy. Never pay anyone who contacts you outside the company's official instructions, and tell us if someone asks you to.

## 5. Publishing opportunities

Companies that publish on Zigex agree to:

- Post only real opportunities they intend to run, with honest descriptions, dates, location and any fee shown clearly.
- Use applicants' information only to review their application and run the opportunity, keep it secure, and not share or sell it.
- Treat applicants fairly and respectfully, and not discriminate unlawfully.
- Reply to applications in a reasonable time, and keep application statuses up to date.
- Never charge students a fee that wasn't shown on the opportunity.

We may review, hide or remove opportunities that don't meet these rules, and remove the "verified" badge from companies that break them.

## 6. Acceptable use

Don't use Zigex to:

- Break the law, or deceive, defraud or impersonate anyone.
- Harass, threaten, bully or discriminate against others.
- Post spam, advertising, pyramid schemes or fake opportunities.
- Collect other people's information, including by copying profiles or using automated tools to read the site.
- Upload viruses or harmful files, or try to break into accounts or our systems.
- Interfere with how Zigex works, or get around limits we put in place.

## 7. What you post

You keep ownership of what you add to Zigex (your profile, documents, photos and answers). You give SEED permission to store, display and share it as needed to run Zigex, for example showing your public profile or sending your application to a company. This permission ends when you delete the content or your account, except where we must keep it for the periods in the Privacy Policy ([How long we keep it](#6-how-long-we-keep-it)).

Only post content you have the right to share. We may remove content that breaks these terms.

The Zigex name, logo and design belong to SEED. Don't use them without our permission.

## 8. Community spaces

Zigex links to community chats (Discord and WhatsApp groups). They're run by those services under their own terms. Be respectful there too; we may remove anyone who breaks these terms from the spaces we manage.

## 9. Changes to Zigex

We keep improving Zigex, so features may change, and some (marked "Soon" or "In development") aren't available yet. We work to keep Zigex running but can't promise it will always be available or free of errors, for example during maintenance or when a provider is down.

## 10. Suspending or closing accounts

You can close your account at any time. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, puts others at risk, or is used for fraud. Where reasonable, we'll tell you why and give you a chance to respond first.

## 11. Our responsibility

Zigex connects students with companies. The companies, not SEED, are responsible for the opportunities they publish, how they select applicants, and how they run internships, programs and events. We check companies before they get the verified badge, but we can't guarantee every opportunity.

To the extent the law allows, SEED isn't liable for indirect losses (such as lost earnings or opportunities) arising from your use of Zigex, or for what companies or other users do. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have by law that can't be limited.

## 12. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of Cameroon. If you have a problem, please contact us first at zigexconnect.com@gmail.com; most issues can be solved quickly. If we can't resolve it, the competent courts of Bamenda will decide.

## 13. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We'll change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you by email or in the app before they apply. If you keep using Zigex after that, the new terms apply.

# Privacy Policy

**Effective 8 October 2026**

This explains what personal information Zigex collects, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how you can see, change or delete it.

## The short version

- We collect what you give us (your account, profile and applications) and the basic technical information needed to run Zigex.
- Other students see your public profile. A company sees your application, documents and phone number only when you apply to it.
- We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
- You can edit your profile at any time and delete your account yourself in Settings.
- Questions or requests: zigexconnect.com@gmail.com.

## 1. Who we are

Zigex is run by **SEED Inc**, Mawa, Mile 6 Nkwen, Bamenda, Cameroon ("SEED", "we", "us"). SEED is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

This policy covers the Zigex website and app at zigexconnect.com, including the version you install on your phone. Companies that publish opportunities on Zigex decide for themselves what they do with the applications they receive; their own privacy policies apply to that.

## 2. What we collect

### When you create an account

- Your name and email address, and a password (stored only in a scrambled, one-way form; we can't read it).
- If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile photo Google shares with us. We never see your Google password.

### Your profile

- What you choose to add: photo, cover image, username, phone number, town, a short introduction, school, course, degree and graduation year, skills, languages, experience, achievements, interests, preferred way of working, and links (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio).
- Optional details some companies ask for: date of birth, grade average, and support you need (for example accessibility needs). Only add these if you want to.

### When you apply or take part

- Your answers on the application, documents you attach (CV, cover letter), and the status of each application.
- If you become an intern: your daily check-ins, reports and tasks. When a company uses QR or location check-in, we record the time and, only at the moment you check in, your location, to confirm you were at the workplace.
- Program and event registrations, and payment acknowledgements for programs with a fee (payments themselves are made to the company, not through Zigex).

### Automatically

- Basic technical information needed to run and secure the service: IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, and error logs.
- Anonymous usage and speed statistics (which pages are viewed, how fast they load). These don't identify you and aren't used for advertising.
- If you turn on notifications: a technical address your browser gives us to deliver them.

## 3. How we use it

We use your information to:

- **Run your account** and keep you signed in.
- **Send your applications** to the companies you choose, and show you their replies.
- **Show you relevant opportunities** and remind you of deadlines and events you registered for.
- **Run internships and programs**: attendance, reports, tasks and certificates, with the company and supervisor responsible.
- **Contact you** about your account, applications and important changes, by email, in the app and (if you turn it on) by notification.
- **Keep Zigex safe**: prevent fraud, spam and misuse, and fix problems.
- **Improve Zigex**, using statistics that don't identify you.

We use your information because you asked us to (your account, applications and the features you use), because you agreed (optional details, notifications), because we have a legitimate interest in running a safe service, or because the law requires it.

**We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.**

## 4. Who can see your information

### Other students and visitors

Your public profile shows your name, photo, cover image, school, course, introduction, skills, experience and links. It never shows your email address, date of birth, grade average or support needs.

### Companies you apply to

When you apply to an internship, program or event, that company sees your profile, your answers, the documents you attached and your phone number, to review your application and contact you. Companies you haven't applied to don't see your phone number or documents.

### Supervisors

If you're an intern, your supervisor and the company see your check-ins, reports and tasks for that internship.

### Service providers

We use trusted providers who process data only on our instructions:

- Hosting and file storage (our servers, Cloudflare, and Supabase for some older images).
- Email delivery (Google Gmail, EmailJS, Resend) and, if you gave a phone number, a WhatsApp messaging provider for a welcome message.
- Notification delivery by your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla).
- Google, for "Continue with Google"; Sanity, for announcements content; and Vercel, for anonymous speed statistics.

Community chats (Discord, WhatsApp groups) are run by those services under their own terms; what you post there is shared with the other members.

### When the law requires it

We may share information if a court or authority lawfully requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

## 5. Cookies and storage on your device

We keep this to what Zigex needs to work:

- **Sign-in cookie** (`zx_access_token`): keeps you signed in. It can't be read by scripts on the page, and it expires on its own.
- **Storage in your browser**: unsent application drafts, whether you dismissed a message, and your notification settings. It stays on your device.
- **App files**: if you install Zigex, your device keeps a copy of some pages so the app opens quickly and can show an offline page.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Blocking the sign-in cookie means you can't sign in.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Your account and profile**: until you delete your account.
- **Applications and their documents**: for 2 years after the opportunity closes, so you and the company can refer back to them, then deleted.
- **Internship records** (check-ins, reports): for 2 years after the internship ends.
- **Inactive accounts**: if you don't sign in for 3 years, we email you and then delete the account if you don't reply within 30 days.
- **Technical logs**: up to 90 days.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can:

- **See and correct** your information at any time in Edit profile.
- **Delete your account** in Settings. This removes your profile, applications, documents and photos.
- **Turn notifications off** in Settings or in your browser.
- **Ask for a copy** of your information, ask us to stop using part of it, or ask a question, by emailing zigexconnect.com@gmail.com.
- **Withdraw an application** from My applications while it's still being reviewed.

We answer requests within 30 days, and may ask you to confirm it's you first. If you think we haven't handled your information properly, you can also complain to the data protection authority in Cameroon.

## 8. Keeping it safe

Connections to Zigex are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored scrambled, the sign-in cookie can't be read by page scripts, CVs and other documents are only shown through short-lived private links, and access to your data is limited to the people who need it.

No service is perfectly secure. Use a password you don't use elsewhere. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 9. Where your data is stored

Some of our providers store data in other countries, including in Europe and the United States. When we use them, we require them to protect your information to the same standard as this policy.

## 10. Age

Zigex is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email zigexconnect.com@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
